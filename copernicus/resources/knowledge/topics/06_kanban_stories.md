The Kanban board is the project management core of COPERNICUS. Stories track features, bugs, and tasks.

**Columns (default):**
- BACKLOG → TODO → IN PROGRESS → REVIEW → DONE

**Creating a story:**
1. Click the **+** button at the top of any column
2. Enter the story title and press Enter
3. The story card appears in that column

**Opening a story:**
- Double-click a card to open the Story Inspector
- The inspector shows: title, description, status, priority, type, epic, acceptance criteria, and linked workflow

**Moving a story:**
- Drag a card to a different column
- Or open the inspector and change the Status field

**Spotlighting a column:**
- Single-click a card to highlight it; click once more on the column header to spotlight the entire column (expands it to fill the board)
- Click the Minimize icon on a column header to collapse it to a bookmark strip

**Story fields:**
- **Priority:** P0 (critical) → P1 (high) → P2 (normal) → P3 (low)
- **Type:** Feature, Bug, Refactor, Docs
- **Epic:** Groups related stories
- **Blocks / Blocked By:** dependency tracking

**Running a story as a workflow:**
- Open a story inspector and click **▶ Run Workflow** to dispatch it through the workflow builder
